Health and biology

Freezing blood for long space trips (CRYO-RESTORE, my M.Sc. project)2026
  • Wrote the M.Sc. proposal: freeze human red blood cells in five ways, thaw them, and measure the damage (cell bursting, oxidation, haemoglobin and energy levels).
  • Built four computer models before going to the lab. They cover damage over time, freezing-agent flow through the cell, energy recovery after thawing, and 1,368 possible protocols.
  • The models showed that washing out the freezing agent is the step that bursts cells. That changed the lab plan. These are model predictions, still to be tested in the lab.
Will the same medicine work in orbit?2026
  • Combined 7 NASA spaceflight studies of mouse livers from NASA's open science data. A key drug-processing gene rose about 30% in 6 of the 7 missions.
  • Linked this to antibiotics. Drugs cleared by that enzyme, such as clarithromycin, may leave the body faster in space. This is mouse gene data, not a human result.
Can a laptop spot drugs that might stop the heart?2026
  • Trained machine-learning models on 13,445 molecules to predict which ones block a heart channel linked to deadly rhythm problems. The best model scored 0.91 (1.0 is perfect).
  • Showed that the usual way of testing flatters the score. On truly new kinds of molecules it fell to 0.85. I wrote a simple check that predicts this gap before training.
Modelling how cancer cells use energy (Project Confluence)2025 to 2026
  • Wrote a mathematical model of cancer-cell metabolism in about 190 Python modules, checked by over 300 automated tests. I fitted it to lab data from 928 cancer cell lines.
  • Later audited my own claim. A single snapshot of lab data pins down only 4 of the 17 unknowns; time-course data would pin down 15. I published that correction.
Heat-risk warnings for Nigeria (El Niño Risk Watch)2026
  • Built a public dashboard for all 36 states and the FCT. It refreshed every 6 hours from five free satellite and weather feeds, to warn people before dangerous heat.
Silver nanoparticles from pawpaw leaves (B.Sc. wet-lab project)2022
  • Made silver nanoparticles from papaya leaf extract and tested them in the lab against two sugar-digesting enzymes linked to diabetes.
  • In 2026 I re-analysed my own data honestly. The plain leaf extract worked better than the nanoparticles, and the standard drug beat both.

Space and engineering

All engine and rocket work is computer design and simulation. Nothing has been printed, built or fired yet.

My own rocket engine (W-2750) and a first test chamber (T01)Oct 2026 to now
  • Designed a large methane and oxygen engine (about 2.75 MN of thrust) from the chemistry up. Every part was generated in code, then stress-checked with finite-element models.
  • The stress checks found three failures that hand sums missed. I fixed them, and every part now passes.
  • Built a computer model of metal 3D printing, checked against public NIST data (7.8% error on a second alloy). I used it to make a print-ready file for a small cooled copper test chamber.
Reusable rocket and electronics2026
  • Modelled a full-size reusable rocket (Wakanda-3) in FreeCAD. A real-geometry check showed the engines could not steer in a 4.2 m body, so I widened it to 5.5 m.
  • Designed engine test-stand electronics in KiCad with ESP32 control code that passes all 4 safety cases in simulation.
  • Designed a 600 mm reusable model rocket (Wakanda 009) and tested it in 2,000 simulated flights.
A chip for spacecraft (WK-1 "Ngao")Nov 2026
  • Designed a small motor-control chip that corrects radiation errors, using free open-source chip tools. It took 1,036 random bit-flips with zero wrong outputs, and passed all layout checks.
