Adaptive Convergence: simulating R&D strategy for a spacefaring civilisation2026
  • Agent-based simulator of long-horizon technology development (procedural technology graphs with competing routes, hidden dead ends, Bayesian cost learning, recursive budget growth); six strategies benchmarked against a perfect-information oracle.
  • Committing to the best option while abandoning projects on statistical evidence of overrun gave +15.8% capability over the best convergent baseline (95% CI 13.0–18.9%, better in 75% of 400 held-out worlds). It cut budget lost to dead ends from 21% to 9% and raised interstellar-milestone success from 41% to 56%.
  • A 20-condition sweep showed the advantage exists only when failures lie outside the planner's model, and ablations showed extra exploration reduced performance. Found and fixed a belief-model bug, then reran all experiments. Replicated March (1991, Organization Science); held-out tuning seeds, bootstrap CIs, Holm-corrected Wilcoxon tests, 21 unit tests.

Rocket Lander RL: reward shaping, reward hacking and robustness2026
  • Trained PPO agents to land a rocket (Gymnasium LunarLander) under four reward designs × 5 seeds, all evaluated on 500 landings with the unshaped reward. The trained agent landed on the pad 99.8% of the time vs. 79.8% for a hand-written control law.
  • Reproduced reward hacking: a per-frame "upright and slow" bonus taught all 5 seeds to hover until timeout (95% of episodes; 4.8% landings). Potential-based shaping (Ng et al., 1999) removed the hack and restored 98.2% landings.
  • Monte Carlo dispersion tests: agents trained only in nominal conditions held 99% landings under heavier gravity (control law 49%), but all methods failed in maximum wind (≤ 21%).
