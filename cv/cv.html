Projects

Teaching an AI to land a rocket2026
  • Trained an AI to land a rocket in a physics simulator. It landed safely on the pad 499 times out of 500; a hand-written control program managed 399.
  • Showed how a badly designed reward makes an AI "cheat": paid for staying upright, it learned to hover forever instead of landing. I fixed the reward, and landings returned to 98%.
  • Tested the AI in strong wind and heavier gravity it had never seen, and reported where it failed as well as where it succeeded.
When should a space programme abandon a plan?2026
  • Built a simulation of a space programme choosing which technologies to develop for the Moon, Mars and beyond. Some paths secretly lead nowhere.
  • Compared six decision strategies across 400 simulated worlds. The best was "commit fully, but quit a project once the evidence shows it is failing". It achieved 16% more progress and cut wasted budget by more than half compared with never quitting.
  • Found and corrected a mistake in my own model, then reran every experiment before reporting results.
Modelling how cancer cells use energy (Project Confluence)
  • Wrote a mathematical model of cancer-cell metabolism and tested it against real laboratory data from 928 cancer cell lines.
  • Using real data instead of made-up data let the model pin down 15 of its 17 unknowns, up from 7. The software is about 43,000 lines of Python, checked by more than 330 automated tests.
