Adaptive Convergence: simulating R&D strategy for a spacefaring civilisation2026
  • Built an agent-based simulator of long-horizon technology development (procedural technology graphs with competing routes, hidden dead ends, Bayesian cost learning, recursive budget growth) and benchmarked six strategies against a perfect-information oracle.
