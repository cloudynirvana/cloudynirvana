### Current Research

- **[Project Confluence](https://github.com/cloudynirvana/project-confluence)** -- A complexity-restoring precision oncology *research* framework (computational / in-silico only; not a medical device or treatment planner). Uses 15-dimensional ODE systems, adaptive therapy controllers, and Bayesian digital twins to model cancer as a dynamical system rather than a static target. In a Monte Carlo sweep across 200 uncertain biological parameter sets, the adaptive policy's resistant-takeover rate was 1/200 (0.5%) versus 178/200 (89%) under MTD -- an in-silico containment metric, not clinical efficacy. [Disclaimer](https://github.com/cloudynirvana/project-confluence/blob/main/DISCLAIMER.md) · [Awaiting clinical validation](https://github.com/cloudynirvana/project-confluence/blob/main/docs/AWAITING_CLINICAL_VALIDATION.md).

- **Metabolic Modeling** -- Investigating cancer metabolism through strain-based computational approaches, with focus on TNBC and multi-pathway metabolic vulnerabilities.
